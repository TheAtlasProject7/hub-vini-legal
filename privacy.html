Hub Vini Pessoal, a personal Enable Banking application

Effective date: 13 November 2026

1. Who operates this application

Hub Vini Pessoal is operated by Vinicius Alves (individual) for his own personal use. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to third parties or the public.

2. Purpose of the application

The application uses Enable Banking solely to obtain read-only access to bank accounts that belong to Vinicius Alves, starting with his own LCL accounts, so that account and transaction information can be shown on his private finance dashboard.

3. Data that may be accessed

With his consent, the application may receive information that the bank and Enable Banking make available for that read-only access. This may include account identifiers, balances, and transaction details. The application does not request payment initiation or any other write access to those accounts.

4. How the data is used

The data is used only to operate and maintain the operator’s private finance dashboard. It is not used for marketing, profiling for third parties, automated decisions about other people, or any purpose unrelated to that personal dashboard.

5. Sharing and sale of data

Account and transaction data obtained through this application is not sold, rented, or otherwise shared with third-party users, advertisers, or unrelated services. Enable Banking and the account-holding bank process data as independent providers in accordance with their own terms and the consent given in the bank’s authentication flow.

6. Storage and retention

Data is stored privately, under the operator’s control, for the purpose described above. It is not published and is not made available to other users. Data is deleted upon request, and may also be deleted when access is revoked or when it is no longer needed for the dashboard.

7. Consent and your rights

Access is based on the account holder’s consent. Consent may be revoked at any time in the bank’s application or in Enable Banking. After revocation, the application will no longer retrieve new data from the revoked connection. The account holder may also request deletion of data already stored for this application.

8. Contact

Privacy and data-protection requests should be sent to the data-protection contact email provided to Enable Banking when this application was registered. That address is not published on this page.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL, with a revised effective date.
